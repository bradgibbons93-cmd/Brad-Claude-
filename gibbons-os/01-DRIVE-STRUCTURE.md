## Where things live (the anti-mess rules)

| Thing | Lives in | Never in |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Documents, PDFs, project files | Google Drive | iCloud Drive |
| Photos & videos (camera roll) | Google Photos (auto-backup on) + Mylio as the curated library | Drive |
| Passwords | Password manager (1Password/Bitwarden) | any document, any AI chat |
| Legal evidence | 03_LEGAL, dated filenames | phone camera roll only |
| AI chat exports worth keeping | 99_ARCHIVE/AI Exports | scattered downloads |

**iCloud's only job:** iPhone backup and device sync. Turn OFF iCloud Drive for
documents so files stop splitting between "iCloud Drive" and "Drive" on your phone.
One cloud for files (Google), one backup for the phone (iCloud). That's the whole fix.

## File naming

`YYYY-MM-DD_what-it-is.ext` → sorts itself chronologically forever.
Non-negotiable in 03_LEGAL; good habit everywhere else.
