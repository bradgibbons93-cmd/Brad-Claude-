# 05 — Capture & Reminders (for a brain that gets distracted)

**Honest call: do not build a reminder app.** You already own the best one — it's on
your wrist/in your pocket, works offline, and pops up on your phone, which is where
you actually are. Building one is another project; using one is a habit.

## The system (three pieces, that's all)

### 1. Capture — Apple Reminders via Siri
Anything crosses your mind, anywhere:
> "Hey Siri, remind me to send Stevie the draft at 4pm"
> "Hey Siri, remind me about Lilly's sick note when I get home"

Zero friction, works while driving, location-aware. This is the "quickly just talk
to it" tool you asked for. It already exists.

Set up four lists in Reminders: **Kids · Cel-X · Legal · Life**. Siri files by name:
"add to my Legal list: export this week's messages."

### 2. Calendar — anything with a date/time
School events, client calls, court dates → Google Calendar, with alerts.
Reminders = tasks. Calendar = appointments. Don't mix them.

### 3. Weekly sort — 20 minutes, Sunday night
- Empty `00_INBOX` in Drive into the right folders.
- Export the week's legal messages/evidence into `03_LEGAL` with dated filenames.
- Look at `06-WEEK-1.md` and set the next week's three finish-targets.

Put the weekly sort itself in Calendar, recurring, with an alert. It's the one
habit that keeps the whole system clean.

## Daily rhythm (keep it this small)

- **Morning (2 min):** look at today's calendar + Reminders. Pick the ONE thing that must get finished.
- **Kids' time is blocked in the calendar like a client meeting.** It doesn't move for work; work moves for it. That's the whole point of the system.
