# 03 — Family & Character Reference System

**Why:** so no AI ever invents a wrong version of Lilly, Skylah, or anyone else.
Every image, avatar, story, or mention pulls from a reference sheet — never from an AI's imagination.

**Where the real sheets live:** `01_FAMILY/Reference Sheets/` in Google Drive — one doc per
person, with their photos alongside. **The filled-in sheets and photos stay in Drive.
This repo only holds the template.** (Rule 5: kids' details don't go in GitHub.)

## People who need a sheet

Brad · Lilly · Skylah · Mim · Riannon · Coby · Laylah · Mum (Granny) · Daniel · Boo ·
Dad · Anita · Sophie · Maddie · Tyson · Carlin · Ariana

Do Brad, Lilly and Skylah first. The rest can be one-liners until needed.

## The template (copy per person)

```
NAME:
CALLED:            (nicknames, what the kids call them)
RELATIONSHIP:      (to Brad)
AGE / BIRTHDAY:
APPEARANCE:        (height, build, hair, eyes, distinguishing features)
PERSONALITY:       (3-5 words)
VOICE / SPEECH:    (how they talk)
LIKES:
DISLIKES:
IMPORTANT CONTEXT: (anything an AI must never get wrong)
REFERENCE PHOTOS:  (link to the photos subfolder in Drive)
APPROVED USES:     (e.g. "family artwork yes, public content no")
DO NOT:            (e.g. "never depict in generated content without asking Brad")
```

## How AIs use it

When generating anything involving a family member, the prompt includes:
*"Use the attached reference sheet and photos for [name]. Do not invent appearance,
personality, or details not on the sheet. If the sheet doesn't cover it, ask."*

**Kids' rule:** Lilly and Skylah's photos and sheets get attached per-task, per-tool,
by Brad — never uploaded into a tool's permanent memory or a public-facing system.
